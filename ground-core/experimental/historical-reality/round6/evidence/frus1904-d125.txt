Mr. Conger to Mr. Hay. American Legation,Peking, China, February 13, 1904. No. 1503.] Sir: I have the honor to confirm your telegram received February 10. The Chinese Government have readily consented, and I have sent telegraphic instructions to Consul Miller to take charge of Japanese interests in his jurisdiction in accordance with your instructions. I have, etc. E. H. Conger.
