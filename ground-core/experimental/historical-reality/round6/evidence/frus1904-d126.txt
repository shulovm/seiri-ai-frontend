Mr. Conger to Mr. Hay. [Telegram.—Paraphrase.] American Legation,Peking, February 14, 1904. (Mr. Conger reports that in a telegraphic communication from Consul Miller he is informed that all Japanese people arriving at Niuchwang from the interior and trying to leave Manchuria are arrested and taken to Port Arthur. The Japanese Government is being informed by its minister here in order that the matter may be presented at St. Petersburg.)
